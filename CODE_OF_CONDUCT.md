# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want participation in HomeStream to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient with people who are new to the project.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or API key, without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a person for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the repository's **Security** tab (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and mark the report as a conduct issue, or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled privately and in good faith, and reporters are protected from retaliation.
